# Deliverable Templates

## Decision Report

1. Executive summary and recommendation.
2. Decision, audience, scope, region, and freshness date.
3. Evidence coverage and known gaps.
4. Three-ring competitor pool and market structure.
5. Comparison matrix and dimension rationale.
6. Representative deep dives and negative cases.
7. Opportunity map and testable hypotheses.
8. Recommended direction, anti-goals, risks, and validation plan.
9. Full competitor, image, and source appendix.

## Visual HTML

- Use a strong executive summary before long analysis.
- Provide sticky or compact navigation for long reports.
- Use responsive tables with horizontal scrolling on small screens.
- Pair every deep-dive title with its mapped hero image and caption.
- Distinguish evidence, hypothesis, decision, risk, and missing evidence visually.
- Embed or package assets predictably; do not leave broken local paths.
- Include print styles and meaningful image alternative text.
- Show image/source coverage in the appendix.

## Slides

- One decision or contradiction per slide.
- Use full matrices in the appendix; surface only decision-driving comparisons in the main story.
- Pair product images with the specific claim they support.
- End with a recommendation, anti-goals, and the next validation test.

## Short Review

For a rapid review, preserve the same logic in five parts: decision, evidence gaps, market pattern, opportunity hypotheses, and recommended next test.
