# Dimension Packs

Select a compact set that can change the decision. Use the universal core, one primary domain pack, and only the supporting dimensions that matter. Do not score every item by default.

## Universal Core

- Target segment and decision maker.
- Core job, trigger, and current workaround.
- Value proposition and proof.
- Primary journey or delivery mechanism.
- Differentiation and substitutability.
- Price, total cost, and business model.
- Distribution, acquisition, and switching cost.
- Trust, risk, and evidence quality.

## Physical Products And Hardware

- Form factor, ergonomics, portability, durability, and maintenance.
- Sensors, inputs, outputs, states, feedback, and attention demand.
- Battery, performance, connectivity, accessories, and repairability.
- Manufacturing complexity, supply chain, certification, and after-sales support.
- Companion software, ecosystem dependence, offline fallback, and service lifetime.

## Software, Apps, And SaaS

- Activation, onboarding, information architecture, and core workflow.
- Collaboration, roles, permissions, administration, and auditability.
- Integrations, import/export, APIs, automation, and data portability.
- Reliability, performance, security, privacy, and compliance.
- Free-to-paid boundary, expansion path, retention mechanism, and switching cost.

## Services

- Service promise, eligibility, booking or entry, delivery, recovery, and support.
- Human versus automated touchpoints and handoff quality.
- Wait time, coverage, consistency, service-level commitment, and exception handling.
- Capacity, labor model, operational leverage, and unit economics.
- Trust signals, guarantees, refund or cancellation, and complaint resolution.

## Platforms And Marketplaces

- Demand-side and supply-side value propositions.
- Liquidity, matching quality, discovery, ranking, and transaction flow.
- Network effects, incentives, governance, moderation, and disintermediation risk.
- Take rate, monetization surfaces, subsidies, and participant economics.
- Trust, identity, reputation, fraud prevention, disputes, and lock-in.

## B2B Solutions

- Buyer, user, administrator, approver, and implementation partner needs.
- Procurement, security review, deployment, migration, training, and change management.
- Integration depth, configurability, data model, permissions, and observability.
- Time to value, total cost of ownership, ROI evidence, and renewal risk.
- Support, SLA, compliance, vendor viability, and exit or data-portability plan.

## Consumer Goods And Retail

- Need state, usage occasion, formulation or material, performance, and sensory experience.
- Packaging, size, variants, SKU architecture, claims, and proof.
- Price ladder, promotion, margin, repeat purchase, and replenishment rhythm.
- Shelf, marketplace, direct-to-consumer, social, and offline channel execution.
- Brand codes, message hierarchy, reviews, trust, regulation, and sustainability.

## Channel And Brand Lens

- Positioning, category frame, promise, reasons to believe, and message consistency.
- Search, content, social, retail, partnerships, sales, and community channels.
- Creative system, product demonstration, review signals, and conversion path.
- Audience fit, acquisition cost, channel dependency, and brand defensibility.

## Mode Selection Examples

- Smart appliance: universal core + physical products + software ecosystem.
- SaaS collaboration tool: universal core + software/SaaS + B2B buying.
- Food delivery marketplace: universal core + platform + service operations.
- Skincare line: universal core + consumer goods/retail + channel/brand.
- Enterprise consulting service: universal core + services + B2B solution.
